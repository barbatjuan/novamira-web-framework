# `STY-NEO-BRUTALIST` — Neo-Brutalist

> Authored in style-catalog PR 4b (`tasks.md` 4b.3) — one of the 3 new v1 entries completing the
> catalog from 5 to 8. See `_README.md` for the font-budget decision behind every new entry's
> typography and the 28-pair separation table this entry was chosen against.

**Axes:** scale `monumental` · ground `saturated` · density `compact` · composition `asymmetric` ·
elevation `none` · accent `polychrome` · chassis `hard-shadow` · ornament `pattern`

**Fits:** Brands that refuse to be tasteful — streetwear drops, youth culture, anything that reads
as a dare. Loud is the entire point here, not a risk somebody is managing down.

**Typography:** `--font-primary` **Archivo** at 700, its native `100%` stretch; `--font-secondary`
**DM Sans** regular. Both SIL OFL. Archivo has only ever carried a secondary role in this catalog
(`STY-DIRECT`, at 400) — pushed to 700 as a PRIMARY here for the first time, and deliberately the
opposite move from `STY-DIRECT`, which widens the same family to `125%` (`Archivo Expanded`) for
polish: this keeps it at native width, narrow and dense, closer to a stencil than a wordmark.
Display tracking `0` (no tightening at all — a brutalist headline does not pretend to have been
considered), wordmark `.05em`.

**Motion intensity:** shortest documented duration, no scale step — a lift alone, sharp and
immediate. The block reads as stamped down, not eased in.

**Imagery:** raw, unretouched, harsh-flash — the exact photograph a considered brand crops out of
its own deck.

**Card recipe:** a hard offset shadow present at rest, never only on hover — the block looks dropped
in place, not laid down gently. No blur, no fade.

The pattern ornament stays at the axis's own low-contrast ceiling on purpose: a quiet hazard-stripe
hatch behind the tag row is the one element on the page that is *not* shouting, which is what makes
it read as a system rather than noise. The accent's one-colour rule is suspended for exactly that
tag/drop-number row (`ACC-POLYCHROME`'s own named-and-bounded exception) — nowhere else on the page
runs more than the one accent.
