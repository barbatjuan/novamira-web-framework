## Why this change existed

Every site the framework produced read as the same site with a different logo, **including its
colour temperature**. Measured, not felt: a hex census over `skills/**` (178 distinct colours, 983
occurrences) found darks `L≤22` were 64% blue, lights `L≥88` 43% blue, and the four most-repeated
hexes — `#0e1113`, `#171b1e`, `#f4f6f7`, `#15181a` — were one cool blue-grey at four lightnesses.

**This was the third attempt.** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-12-design-personalities-design.md`
and `2026-08-14-perceptual-axes-design.md` are the first two; the second opens by stating the
first's diagnosis was incomplete, and the complaint outlived it.

**What made this one different:** both prior attempts began with a catalog. This one began with the
delivery path, because a client site never reached the generator — it copied one of two
hand-maintained chassis and re-pointed six lines, so radius, easing, button padding, section
padding, eyebrow tracking, card recipe and hover zoom were byte-identical across every client. The
catalog was Slice 4, not Slice 1.

## OPEN RISK 1 — the static chassis default

`$CHASSIS_STYLE_BY_SITE` maps `corporate → institutional` and `ecommerce → matter`. That is
**verbatim the pair `skills/html-mockup/references/mockup-guide.md:436-447` named as the historical
defect**: *"every corporate project shipped `PERS-INSTITUTIONAL` and every commerce one
`PERS-MATTER`, not because anyone chose them but because nobody was asked to."*

Mitigated, not removed. The intake now asks, `es_record_style_resolution()` persists the answer,
`RT_MOCKUP_AXES_MISMATCH` checks the delivered mockup by value, and
`RT_STYLE_UNRESOLVED_DEFAULT` FAILs a ledger row that names a chassis with no style. But the
default itself stands. **If sites start reading alike again, look here first.**

## OPEN RISK 2 — eight styles share five faces

No new SIL OFL face was embedded. The session had no network access to verify a face's source,
checksum and licence, which `assets/fonts/_fonts.php`'s own header requires before a woff2 ships.
The proposal budgeted ~6 new faces. Typographic separation is therefore **thinner than planned**,
and `RT_MOCKUP_FONT_NOT_EMBEDDED` will FAIL any style that names an unembedded family.

## Partially met — 3b.7

## Four orchestrator errors, corrected mid-flight

Recorded because a report that hides its own mistakes is the same defect this change spent six
slices removing.

1. **An invariant carried out of its domain.** *"`index.html` byte-identical"* was Slice 1's proof
   for a refactor that must not change output. It was carried into Slice 2, which changes the design
   system on purpose, making the PR impossible to satisfy. Discovered by an executor with 132 FAILs
   of evidence. Retired from Slice 2 onward.
2. **A mis-specified exit criterion, defended for twelve PRs.** It demanded the chassis anchor
   resolve from the selected style. `_build-gallery.php` has no WordPress bootstrap and no
   per-project identity — there is no project at generation time. And the real defect was never the
   default's value; `mockup-guide.md` says *"because nobody was asked"*. **It was the silence.**
   Corrected in `tasks.md` with the evidence.
3. **`git add skills/` swept the user's untracked `blind-judges/` into a commit** — the exact rule
   given explicitly to seven consecutive executors. Reverted with `reset --soft` + `restore --staged`.
4. **A gate that did the opposite of its stated purpose.** `RT_STYLE_PRECHARGE_UNSHIPPED` first
   compared exact values, which would have FAILed a client for overriding a precharged toggle —
   precisely the choice `toggles.md` offers them. A gate against sameness enforcing uniformity: the
   defect rebuilt inside its own cure. Corrected to a presence check, with two new fixtures.
